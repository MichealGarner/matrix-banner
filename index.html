Our Story

A community sauna for the Cornish coast

Born from a love of wild swimming and ancient bathing traditions, Loe Sauna sits at the edge of Loe Beach on the beautiful Fal Estuary. We built this sauna for the community — a place to gather, recover, and reconnect with the natural world around you.

Each session is 45 minutes of pure, dry heat. Step outside and cool in the sea breeze. Come alone, with friends, or meet fellow sauna lovers on the shore.

The Experience

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Wood-fired heat

Kiln-dried birch keeps our sauna at a steady 75–85°C. Dry, enveloping warmth that eases muscle tension from the first minute.

Sea air & views

Step outside between rounds and breathe the salt air of the Fal Estuary. The contrast of heat and cool coastal breeze is restorative.

Inclusive sessions

Sessions welcome all abilities. Small groups of up to 8 keep things intimate. Private hire is available for special occasions.

Easy to find

Located at Loe Beach, Feock — just off the A39 south of Truro. Ample parking. A short beautiful walk to the water's edge.
